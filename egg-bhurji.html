Spicy & Tasty Egg Bhurji

A quick and delicious Indian-style scrambled egg recipe made with onions, tomatoes, green chilli and aromatic spices.

Prep Time 5 Minutes
Cook Time 10 Minutes
Servings 2 People
View Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 Eggs
  • 1 Medium Onion - finely chopped
  • 1 Medium Tomato - finely chopped
  • 1 Green Chilli - finely chopped
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Turmeric Powder
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Red Chilli Powder
  • 1/4 Teaspoon Garam Masala
  • Salt as per taste
  • 2 Tablespoons Oil or Butter
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves

How to Make Egg Bhurji

01

Prepare the Eggs

Break all the eggs into a bowl and whisk them properly. Keep aside.

02

Heat the Pan

Heat oil or butter in a pan on medium flame.

03

Cook Onion and Chilli

Add chopped onion and green chilli. Cook until the onion becomes soft and light golden.

04

Add Tomato and Spices

Add chopped tomato, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, garam masala and salt. Cook for a few minutes.

05

Add the Eggs

Pour the whisked eggs into the pan and keep stirring continuously.

06

Cook the Bhurji

Scramble and cook the eggs until they are soft and properly cooked.

07

Serve Hot

Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with bread, pav or roti.

Recipe Information

Category Breakfast

Difficulty Easy

Total Time 15 Minutes

Servings 2 People

💡 Cooking Tip

Do not overcook the eggs. Keep stirring on medium flame for soft and delicious Egg Bhurji.
