Simple & Tasty Upma

A quick, healthy and delicious South Indian breakfast made with roasted semolina, vegetables and aromatic spices.

Prep Time 10 Minutes
Cook Time 15 Minutes
Servings 2 People
View Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Semolina (Rava / Suji)
  • 1 Medium Onion - finely chopped
  • 1 Green Chilli - finely chopped
  • 1 Small Tomato - chopped
  • 1/4 Cup Green Peas
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Mustard Seeds
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Cumin Seeds
  • 8-10 Curry Leaves
  • 2 Tablespoons Oil
  • 2 Cups Water
  • Salt as per taste
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 1 Tablespoon Lemon Juice

How to Make Upma

01

Roast the Rava

Heat a pan and dry roast the semolina on low flame until it becomes light and aromatic. Keep it aside.

02

Prepare the Tadka

Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds and curry leaves.

03

Cook the Vegetables

Add onion, green chilli, tomato and green peas. Cook until the vegetables become soft.

04

Add Water

Add water and salt. Bring the water to a gentle boil.

05

Add Roasted Rava

Slowly add the roasted rava while continuously stirring to avoid lumps.

06

Cook Properly

Cover and cook for a few minutes until the Upma becomes soft and absorbs all the water.

07

Serve Hot

Add lemon juice and fresh coriander. Serve your delicious homemade Upma hot.

Recipe Information

Category Breakfast

Difficulty Easy

Total Time 25 Minutes

Servings 2 People

💡 Cooking Tip

Add the roasted rava slowly while stirring continuously. This will help you make smooth and lump-free Upma.
