Quick & Tasty Bread Omelette

A quick and delicious breakfast made with eggs, fresh vegetables and soft bread. Perfect for a busy morning.

Prep Time 5 Minutes
Cook Time 10 Minutes
Servings 2 People
View Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 Bread Slices
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Small Onion - finely chopped
  • 1 Small Tomato - finely chopped
  • 1 Green Chilli - finely chopped
  • 2 Tablespoons Fresh Coriander
  • Salt as per taste
  • 1/4 Teaspoon Black Pepper
  • 1 Tablespoon Butter or Oil

How to Make Bread Omelette

01

Prepare the Egg Mixture

Break the eggs into a bowl and whisk them properly.

02

Add Vegetables and Spices

Add onion, tomato, green chilli, coriander, salt and black pepper. Mix everything well.

03

Heat the Pan

Heat a pan on medium flame and add butter or oil.

04

Cook the Omelette

Pour the egg mixture into the pan and spread it gently.

05

Add Bread

Place the bread slices on top of the omelette and press gently.

06

Flip and Cook

Carefully flip the omelette and cook the other side until golden and properly cooked.

07

Serve Hot

Cut into pieces and serve hot with tomato ketchup or your favorite sauce.

Recipe Information

Category Breakfast

Difficulty Easy

Total Time 15 Minutes

Servings 2 People

💡 Cooking Tip

Cook the omelette on medium flame so that the egg cooks properly without burning.
