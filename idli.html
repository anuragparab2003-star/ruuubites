Soft & Delicious Idli

Soft, fluffy and healthy South Indian Idli served as a perfect homemade breakfast with chutney and sambar.

Prep Time 10 Minutes
Cook Time 15 Minutes
Servings 2 People
View Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Idli Rice
  • 1 Cup Urad Dal
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Fenugreek Seeds
  • Salt as per taste
  • Water as required
  • Oil for greasing the Idli mould

How to Make Idli

01

Soak the Ingredients

Wash and soak the Idli rice, urad dal and fenugreek seeds separately for several hours.

02

Grind the Batter

Grind the soaked urad dal and rice into a smooth batter using water as required.

03

Mix and Ferment

Mix both batters properly, add salt and leave the batter to ferment overnight.

04

Prepare the Idli Mould

Grease the Idli mould lightly with oil and pour the prepared batter into each mould.

05

Steam the Idlis

Steam the Idlis for about 10 to 12 minutes until they become soft and fluffy.

06

Serve Hot

Remove the Idlis carefully and serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

Recipe Information

Category Breakfast

Difficulty Easy

Total Time 25 Minutes

Servings 2 People

💡 Cooking Tip

Make sure the batter is properly fermented. This will help the Idlis become soft and fluffy.
