Delicious Homemade Poha

A quick, healthy and delicious Indian breakfast made with flattened rice, onions, peanuts and fresh spices.

Prep Time 10 Minutes
Cook Time 15 Minutes
Servings 2 People
View Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Poha (Flattened Rice)
  • 1 Medium Onion - finely chopped
  • 1 Green Chilli - finely chopped
  • 1 Small Potato - chopped
  • 1/4 Cup Peanuts
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Mustard Seeds
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Turmeric Powder
  • 8-10 Curry Leaves
  • Salt as per taste
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 1 Tablespoon Lemon Juice

How to Make Poha

01

Wash the Poha

Wash the poha gently under water and keep it aside for a few minutes.

02

Prepare the Tadka

Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds, curry leaves and peanuts.

03

Cook the Vegetables

Add onion, green chilli and potato. Cook until the potato becomes soft.

04

Add Spices

Add turmeric powder and salt. Mix everything properly.

05

Add the Poha

Add the washed poha and mix gently. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes.

06

Serve

Add lemon juice and fresh coriander. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious Poha.

Recipe Information

Category Breakfast

Difficulty Easy

Total Time 25 Minutes

Servings 2 People

💡 Cooking Tip

Do not soak the poha for too long. Wash it gently to keep it soft and fluffy.
